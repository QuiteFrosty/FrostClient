Why FrostClient?

Everything you'd expect from a premium client, without the price tag or the closed source.

🗂️

Dark, Grid-Based Mod Menu

Category tabs, live search, and per-module config panels keep every setting a click away.

🎛️

HUD Editor

Drag-and-drop layout editor with shareable Base64 config strings — build your HUD and send it to friends.

⚔️

PvP & Combat Tools

CPS counter, combo counter, reach display, hitboxes, and more built for competitive play.

⚡

FPS Optimizations

Particle culling, HUD render caching, and reduced GC pressure keep frame times low.

🛡️

Crash-Safe Architecture

Soft @Inject Mixin hooks and per-module kill-switches for real compatibility with other Forge mods.

👁️

Fully Inspectable

No black boxes. Fork it, audit it, or contribute to it — the entire client is on GitHub.
